The image in 3D

When you turn the brightness of a normal photograph into height, you get a distorted mess: the nose sinks, the shadows rise, the background bulges. When you do the same with the Shroud, you get a coherent face and body. Compare them yourself. Both views below use exactly the same method.

Fair warning: our 3D views are a simple re-creation of the VP-8 idea, smoothed for the web. They show the principle. They are not a scientific measurement.

About these photographs

The full-cloth images here are public domain photographs. The finest photographs of the Shroud, taken by Barrie Schwortz for the 1978 scientific team, and the very high resolution scans from 2002 and 2008, are protected by copyright. You can see many of them at shroud.com and shroudphotos.com. We have asked for permission to show them here.
